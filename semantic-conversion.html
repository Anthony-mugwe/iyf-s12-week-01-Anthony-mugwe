What Is Semantic HTML?

Semantic HTML uses elements that describe the meaning and purpose of their content.

Examples of Semantic Elements

  • header: introductory content
  • nav: navigation links
  • main: the main content of a page
  • article: a self-contained piece of content
  • aside: related or supplementary content
  • footer: closing information

Why Semantic HTML Matters

Semantic elements help browsers, developers, search engines, and assistive technologies understand a webpage's structure.

Remember

Choose HTML elements based on their meaning, not merely how you want them to look.
